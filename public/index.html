Frequently asked questions

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API?

No. You link your existing WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business number by scanning a QR code, like WhatsApp Web.

Can my number get blocked?

WhatsApp can restrict numbers that send unwanted bulk messages. Message only customers who opted in. Our Block Risk screen, warm-up limits and auto-pause help you stay safe, but we can't guarantee WhatsApp's decisions.

What happens when my trial ends?

Your data stays safe. Sending and chatbots pause until you choose a plan. Pay by UPI or bank transfer and we activate your plan.

Can my team use it together?

Yes. Add users as admins, managers or agents, group them into departments, and route chats to the right team automatically.

Is my data private?

Each business has its own isolated workspace. Only your team can see your contacts and chats.
